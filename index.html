The single highest-leverage move available

Six schools sit at 0% purely on IELTS: CMU, Oxford, Cambridge, Georgia Tech, Purdue, plus UIUC full status. Moving 7.0 → 7.5 converts those into roughly 30% / 5% / 6% / 30% / 30% / 35%. Two constraints: One Skill Retake only works within 60 days of a computer-delivered sitting, and Edinburgh does not accept One Skill Retake scores at all. Do not let a retake delay the 15 September USC filing — 7.0 already clears USC.
